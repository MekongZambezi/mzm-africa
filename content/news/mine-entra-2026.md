MZM was represented at Mine Entra 2026, Zimbabwe's mining, engineering and transport exhibition, held in Bulawayo from 29 to 31 July 2026.

Mine Entra is now in its 28th edition. It is organised by the Zimbabwe International Trade Fair Company with the Chamber of Mines of Zimbabwe and the Ministry of Mines and Mining Development. This year's theme was "Unearth, Transform, Prosper: Anchoring Economic Transformation Through Mining Value Chains", with a focus on mineral beneficiation, value addition and local supplier development.

That focus matches MZM's own. MZM works on investment and trade between Zimbabwe and Vietnam. In mining, it brings in investors whose plans include processing in Zimbabwe, in line with the 2026 processing requirements and critical minerals framework.

The MZM team in Bulawayo used the exhibition to meet title holders, producers, equipment suppliers and service providers.

Title holders and investors who would like to discuss a mining project can contact projects@mzmafrica.com.
